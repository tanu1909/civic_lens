<h1 align="center">CivicLens 🛰️</h1>

<p align="center">
  CivicLens is an AI-based civic reporting platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and authorities to simplify city maintenance.
  By leveraging Google's Gemini AI, the platform transforms simple photos of infrastructure issues into actionable, prioritized data for government response.

## 🚩 The Problem
Traditional reporting systems suffer from a lack of operational coordination, manual delays, and a lack of transparency. This results in **“dark spots”**—unaddressed infrastructure defects like broken street lights and potholes—that pose serious hazards to citizen security and mobility, particularly for women at night.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧠 Intelligent Reporting
Users simply upload a picture of a problem.

### 🤖 AI Analysis
Uses **Gemini AI** to automatically identify the hazard type and assign a **Severity Score (1–10)** to prioritize urgent issues like exposed manholes over cosmetic problems.

### 🗺 Geospatial Command Center
A live dashboard for government authorities featuring an interactive map with precise geolocations for efficient response times.

### 🛡 Spam Filtering
Automated analysis filters out irrelevant reports to ensure data credibility.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

### Backend & Infrastructure
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)

- **Authentication:** Firebase Auth (Google Sign-In)
